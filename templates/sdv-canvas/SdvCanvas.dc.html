OEMs Must Embrace Re-Engineering of Systems, Processes, & Products

Vehicle software must operate across diverse hardware platforms; integrate with advanced semiconductor architectures; and function reliably across multiple domains such as advanced driver assistance systems (ADAS), infotainment, powertrain, and central compute systems.

To close the gap between traditional, hardware-centric development cycles and the need for continuous software-driven development, original equipment manufacturers (OEMs) must embrace re-engineering of systems, processes, and products.

Virtual-first development strategies across silicon, software, and systems improve efficiency, scalability, and performance, thus enabling continuous integration and optimization across the entire vehicle lifecycle.
